shippori-mincho-b1-latin-400.woff2 and shippori-mincho-b1-latin-700.woff2 are
the Latin subsets of Shippori Mincho B1 as served by Google Fonts.

dotgothic16-latin.woff2 is a subset of DotGothic16-Regular.ttf that keeps
Latin, punctuation, arrows, box drawing, geometric shapes, the black star and
the katakana middle dot.

DotGothic16: https://github.com/fontworks-fonts/DotGothic16
